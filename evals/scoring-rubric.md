# Scoring Rubric

## 5 — Exceptional
Specific, authored, technically correct, restrained, visually coherent, target-aware.

## 4 — Professional
Strong overall result with minor weaknesses.

## 3 — Competent
Works and is coherent, but contains generic or under-resolved decisions.

## 2 — Weak
Technically functional but conceptually generic, repetitive or poorly targeted.

## 1 — Poor
Mostly decorative effect stacking or technical mismatch.

## 0 — Failure
Does not implement the brief or cannot render.

## Critical reject conditions

Automatic reject if:
- wrong target dimensions
- unusable alpha
- broken loop
- missing required layer separation
- copied reference
- unsupported transport claimed as direct
- no visual inspection
- non-deterministic generative behavior
